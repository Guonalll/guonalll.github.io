Biography

Naliang Guo is a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Geographic Sciences and Natural Resources Research, Chinese Academy of Sciences. He received his Bachelor's degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from China University of Geosciences, Wuhan, and completed his doctoral training in Cartography and Geographic Information Systems.

His research focuses on flood risk assessment, emergency management, agricultural resource and environmental systems, and spatial modeling. His work integrates geospatial analysis, agent-based modeling, system dynamics, and data-driven approaches to support environmental governance, disaster response, and territorial spatial planning.
